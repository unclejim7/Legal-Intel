◼ Illinois Spotlight
Reversed
Subway Franchise v. IWCC
The Illinois Appellate Court reversed the IWCC on a repetitive motion injury claim, finding the Commission had applied too narrow a standard for occupational causation in a food service worker's carpal tunnel case. The court found the treating physician's opinion, supported by ergonomic evidence, was sufficient to establish causation notwithstanding an IME opinion to the contrary.
Affirmed
Concrete Structures Inc. v. IWCC
The Appellate Court affirmed the Commission's award in a construction worker spinal injury claim, upholding a finding of accident arising out of employment where the petitioner demonstrated the injury occurred during a work-required physical task. The court rejected the employer's "personal comfort doctrine" deviation argument.
Reversed
Heiden v. Westmont School District
The Appellate Court reversed a denial of TTD benefits in an educational worker claim, finding the IWCC had failed to adequately consider the petitioner's inability to perform modified duty work in the context of available light-duty positions that exceeded her physical restrictions.

The Carrier Surveillance Stack: What Defense Is Running Against Your Clients

Insurance carriers defending WC and PI claims have built a multi-layer AI surveillance architecture that most plaintiff attorneys are not systematically countering in discovery. The current stack includes: Colossus successors (AI claim valuation matrices ingesting treatment codes, diagnostic data, and jurisdiction-specific settlement history); social media monitoring AI (NLP tools that flag inconsistencies between reported functional limitations and observable activity); vehicle telematics (for auto PI cases, accident reconstruction AI pulling black box and GPS data); and wearable/IoT data aggregation (health data from claimant-owned devices increasingly used in causation disputes).

The plaintiff-side response requires updated discovery demands that specifically request: model documentation for any AI valuation tool used; training data provenance; the specific inputs that generated the carrier's reserve or offer; and any surveillance report generated by AI or algorithmic tools. If you're not asking for this, you're litigating against a black box.

📖 Word of the Day
Subrogation (n.) — The legal right of an insurer or employer to step into the shoes of the injured claimant and pursue recovery against a third-party tortfeasor after paying workers' compensation benefits. In Illinois, governed by 820 ILCS 305/5(b); the employer's lien attaches to any third-party recovery and is the central battleground in dual-recovery WC/tort cases.
