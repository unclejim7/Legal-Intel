📚 Legal Fact of the Day
The billable hour, now ubiquitous in American legal practice, was not widely adopted until the 1950s and 1960s. Before that era, most attorneys charged flat fees or percentage-of-recovery arrangements. The ABA's 1958 report recommending hourly billing as the standard helped institutionalize it — making today's AI-driven disruption of the billable hour a return to models that predated the current standard by decades.
📖 Word of the Day
Subrogation (n.) — The substitution of one party for another with respect to a lawful claim or right. In WC practice, the employer's insurer who pays WC benefits steps into the shoes of the injured worker to pursue a third-party tortfeasor who caused the injury. Lien resolution and subrogation negotiation are among the highest-value AI automation opportunities in WC practice — and among the most underserved by current tools.
