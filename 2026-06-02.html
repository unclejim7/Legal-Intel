◼ FROM THE LEGAL DESK
⚖️ Legal Fact
The Illinois Workers' Compensation Act was originally enacted in 1911, making Illinois one of the earliest states to adopt a comprehensive workers' compensation system. The Act was designed as a "grand bargain" — employees surrendered common law tort rights against employers in exchange for guaranteed, fault-free benefits; employers surrendered tort defenses in exchange for limited, predictable liability. That bargain is now 115 years old, and AI is the first technology since the advent of claims adjusting software to fundamentally alter the economics on both sides simultaneously.
📖 Word of the Day
Subrogation (n.) — The substitution of one party for another with respect to a legal claim or right. In Illinois WC practice, the employer or carrier who pays WC benefits acquires subrogation rights against any third party whose negligence contributed to the injury — allowing recovery of paid benefits from the tortfeasor. Under 820 ILCS 305/5(b), Illinois follows a modified Kotecki lien approach that caps employer subrogation recovery at the employer's actual WC expenditure. AI-powered lien management tools are now automating subrogation lien calculations that previously required paralegal hours.
