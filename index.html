The Illinois Workers' Compensation Act (820 ILCS 305/) has been continuously in force since 1911, making it one of the oldest workers' compensation statutes in the United States. Illinois was among the first wave of states to adopt a compulsory WC system following the Triangle Shirtwaist Factory fire of 1911, which killed 146 workers and triggered a national push for mandatory employer liability reform.
